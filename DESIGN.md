## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a dignified, transparent, and approachable digital interface for municipal governance along the highland slopes of Mount Lawu. The personality balances institutional authority (*Pamong Praja*) with rural warmth, community solidarity (*Gotong Royong*), and civic accessibility.

The visual narrative draws from modern institutional clarity, tempered by agricultural earthiness. It avoids cold technocratic minimalism and bureaucratic stiffness in favor of a clean, humanist civic layout. Surfaces are welcoming, content is highly legible for multi-generational residents, and interactive targets are generously proportioned for universal utility across low-bandwidth mobile devices and public access terminals.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional credibility through grounded botanical tones paired with warm civic amber:

- **Primary (`#4a5d23` / Hijau Pamong):** Rooted in the traditional olive green of civic administration and the volcanic agricultural terraces of Mount Lawu. Applied to primary navigational bars, structural headers, active states, and core verification badges.
  - Deep tone (`#3b4b1c`): Primary hover states, high-contrast text on light surfaces, and institutional emblems.
  - Light accent tone (`#5c742c`): Interactive focus borders, secondary highlight cards, and progress markers.
- **Secondary (`#d97706` / Emas Agrikultur):** Evokes golden harvest, civic announcements, public services, and key callouts. Used deliberately on action buttons, alert banners, and service badges.
  - Warm support (`#fef3c7`): Soft tinted background for urgent public notices, announcements, and subtle status tags.
- **Neutral & Surfaces:**
  - Base white (`#ffffff`): Card surfaces, modal sheets, and primary document containers.
  - Surface subtle (`#f9fafb` to `#f3f4f6`): Global body background, table alternations, and secondary form containers.
  - Body text (`#1f2937`): High-legibility neutral dark for all long-form reading, governance circulars, and civic data tables.
  - Border subtle (`#e5e7eb`): Gentle structural separation maintaining airiness without visual clutter.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the primary typeface for headers and running civic copy, celebrating contemporary Indonesian municipal identity with balanced humanist proportions and open counters. Inter is utilized across labels, tags, tabular records, numerical datasets, and administrative forms to ensure absolute precision and visual clarity.

Headings feature tight tracking to convey institutional weight without feeling distant. Body text maintains relaxed vertical rhythm to enhance reading comfort across lengthy village bylaws (*Peraturan Desa*), transparency reports, and community procedures.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column grid system capped at a maximum width of 1200px for optimal reading density on wide displays. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and dynamic margins centered in the viewport. Administrative portals utilize a 3-column sticky sidebar with a 9-column content viewport.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px outer margins. Multi-column cards collapse from 4-across to 2-across.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 16px lateral padding. Service links convert to scrollable horizontal trays or full-width touch cards.

An 8px base rhythm dictates internal element padding, card separations, and vertical composition to retain a disciplined, civic structural balance.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation conveys order, legitimacy, and interaction affordance through subtle tinted ambient shadows rather than harsh drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for background canvas (`#f9fafb`) and secondary administrative containers. Surrounded by a 1px border (`#e5e7eb`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`. Applied to informational cards, directory lists, and standard panels on top of the canvas.
- **Level 2 (Dropdown & Interactive Hover):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`. Used for active hover states, public service cards, and search auto-suggest menus.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Critical Notices):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Reserved for civic inquiry modals, official document previews, and emergency community bulletins.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a balanced rounded style (`0.5rem` / `8px` base) reflecting a modern, community-oriented civic presence that avoids both clinical sharp edges and overly informal roundness:

- **Interactive controls & inputs:** `8px` (`rounded-md` equivalent) for form fields, action buttons, and dropdown triggers.
- **Content cards & containers:** `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to establish friendly, distinct content zones for announcements, leader profiles, and village statistics.
- **Badges, status tags & pill chips:** Full circular radius (`9999px`) to create clear semantic differentiation from rectangular data and form controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Civic Button:** Solid `#4a5d23` background with `#ffffff` text, 8px border radius, 44px min-height for accessibility. Hover state shifts to `#3b4b1c` with smooth 150ms transition.
- **Action / Service Button:** Solid `#d97706` background with `#ffffff` text. Dedicated to citizen requests (e.g., *Pengajuan Surat*, *Layanan Warga*).
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent fill with 1.5px solid `#4a5d23` border and `#4a5d23` text. Hover introduces `#f3f4f6` fill.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Form inputs feature white backgrounds, 1px `#d1d5db` border, and 8px corner radius with 12px 16px internal padding.
- Focused state applies 1.5px solid border in `#5c742c` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(92, 116, 44, 0.15)`).
- Error states transition border to `#dc2626` with red assistive helper text directly beneath.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#ffffff` fill, 12px border radius, subtle Level 1 ambient elevation, and 1px `#e5e7eb` outer border.
- Highlight cards (e.g., Village Announcements / *Warta Desa*) feature a 4px solid top accent in `#4a5d23` or `#d97706`.

### Chips & Badges
- Administrative status badges (e.g., *Selesai*, *Dalam Proses*, *Pemberitahuan*) use pill shapes with 4px vertical and 12px horizontal padding.
- Colors pair light tint backgrounds with high-contrast text:
  - Success/Active: `#ecfdf5` background with `#065f46` text.
  - Notice/Pending: `#fef3c7` background with `#92400e` text.
  - Civic Official: `#f0fdf4` background with `#4a5d23` text and 1px border `#dcfce7`.

### Lists & Tables
- Village demographic records, budget transparency metrics (*APBDes*), and official agendas use alternating `#ffffff` and `#f9fafb` row fills.
- Row separators use crisp 1px `#f3f4f6` borders with 12px vertical padding, ensuring effortless scanning for citizens on both desktop screens and mobile devices.

### Specialized Civic Components
- **Bansos & Service Status Tracker:** Progress indicator with connected steps using `#4a5d23` for completed stages and `#d97706` for current in-review statuses.
- **Official Seal Banner:** Header bar showcasing municipal crest, official nomenclature, and active operational status of the village hall (*Kantor Desa*).
